CQ7: Which components have no monitoring data? (incomplete information)
======================================================================
Components with no monitoredBy link (Python check): ['order_queue']
    expected: ['order_queue']
Why Python and not the reasoner: under the open world assumption, a missing
monitoredBy link means 'unknown', not 'none'. The reasoner cannot conclude
that order_queue is unmonitored, because a tool might exist that was never
recorded. Treating missing data as absent is a closed-world application choice.
